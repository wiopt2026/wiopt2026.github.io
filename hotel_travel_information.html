Travel Information

By Air

  • John Glenn Columbus International Airport (CMH)
    • 15 – 20 mins to EAIC by Uber or taxi
  • Rickenbacker International Airport (LCK)
    • 25 – 30 mins to EAIC by Uber or taxi

By Car

  • Parking is available on the west side of the EAIC building
  • Parking fees apply (payable via ParkMobile or ParkColumbus mobile apps)
